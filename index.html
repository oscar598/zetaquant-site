How it works

Unlock apps productively, using Zeta math.

You already open these apps dozens of times a day. Zeta uses Apple's Screen Time API to put a short round of math in front of each one.

01
You open a blocked app

Instagram, TikTok, or anything you pick with Apple's Screen Time picker.

02
f(x)
Math first. Solve 5.

The next problem appears the moment your answer is right. No enter key.

03
Unlocked for 10 minutes

Then the shield comes back on its own, even if Zeta isn't running.

Practice ahead to bank time: 10 correct answers = 1 minute. Question count (1–40) and unlock length (1–60 min) are yours to set.

What's Zeta?

Just single- and double-digit mental math.

No calculus, no brain teasers. The same four operations as zetamac, the arithmetic test quant candidates practice on. Answer as many as you can in 120 seconds.

+47 + 382–100 + 2–100
−91 − 47Addition, reversed
×8 × 632–12 × 2–100
÷336 ÷ 7Multiplication, reversed

Every finished run ends on a card with your score, rank, and school, ready to share.
